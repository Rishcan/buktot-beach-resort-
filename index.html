About Blue Sea Tourism

More than a destination. A meaningful island experience.

Blue Sea Tourism is a student-developed tourism website concept that introduces travelers to the natural beauty, outdoor adventures, and cultural richness of Mindoro in the Philippines. We aim to help visitors plan thoughtfully and travel responsibly.

Our mission is to share helpful destination information and inspire safe, respectful, and memorable journeys. Our vision is to encourage tourism that values local communities, heritage, and the environment.

✓ Nature and beach escapes

Who is it for? Families, couples, student travelers, groups of friends, and nature lovers looking for a Mindoro getaway.

Plan your route

Getting to Mindoro

Choose your arrival town and confirm the latest ferry, bus, road, and local transport schedules before travel.

Travel basics

  • From Metro Manila: Common routes use road transport to a Batangas port, then a ferry to a Mindoro port such as Puerto Galera or Calapan, depending on your destination.
  • Within Mindoro: Buses, vans, tricycles, and local transport may be available depending on the town and time of day.
  • Travel time: Varies with origin, ferry schedule, traffic, sea conditions, and onward road travel. Check operators for current estimates.
  • Nearby experiences: Beaches, diving areas, waterfalls, lakes, markets, local food, and community-led cultural activities.
  • Stay and dine: Compare licensed accommodation and local dining options near your chosen route; confirm availability and prices directly.

Travel smarter

Your Mindoro travel guide

A little preparation helps protect you, the communities you visit, and the places that make Mindoro special.

☀️

Best time to visit

Generally, the drier months can be more convenient for outdoor trips, but tropical weather changes. Check the forecast and marine advisories before departure.

🎒

What to bring

Reusable water bottle, sun protection, insect repellent, comfortable shoes, light clothing, swimwear, basic medicines, and a waterproof bag.

🛟

Safety first

Follow lifeguard and guide instructions, wear a life jacket when required, avoid risky swimming, and do not hike or boat during unsafe conditions.

🌿

Leave no trace

Take rubbish with you, avoid single-use plastics where possible, do not touch or collect corals and wildlife, and stay on permitted trails.

🤝

Respect local culture

Ask permission before photographing people, dress appropriately at cultural or religious sites, and follow community guidance. Engage with Mangyan communities only through respectful, authorized experiences.

♿

Accessibility & budget

Confirm stairs, terrain, restroom access, and transport needs with providers. Set aside funds for transport, meals, fees, accommodation, and emergency expenses.

Emergency reminder: For urgent help, contact the local emergency services, police, coast guard, or your accommodation/operator. Save local contacts before traveling. This student website does not provide live emergency dispatch or real-time weather updates.
